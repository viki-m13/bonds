The strategy

Buy when a bond prints ≥3 pts below its own trailing 60-day median (a forced-seller dislocation) and it clears a trailing-liquidity gate. Fill at the next customer-buy print. Hold ~1 year; exit into a customer-sell print. Coupon accrues throughout.

The dealer bid–ask spread (~0.44 pt round-trip) kills short-term timing but is a one-time cost against growing carry + reversion. Buying idiosyncratic dislocations and holding to reversion beats random entry in the same bonds — pure timing alpha, net of spread, carry and selection.

Known failure mode — sustained rate selloffs

Mean-reversion is short a rate shock. In 2022 the whole market repriced down and dislocations did not revert (−1.99% excess). Size accordingly; an optional MUB-trend overlay mitigates it.
