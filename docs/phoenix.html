Methodology, honestly

  • Booking convention. Every sleeve books the value dated t as the P&L realized over open[t−1]→open[t] from a position decided on information through close[t−2]. One convention everywhere — cross-sleeve correlations, the blend NAV and the overlay are all computed on contemporaneous, executable returns.
  • Allocation. Equal weight over active sleeves. Zero estimated parameters. The v3 walk-forward allocator (trailing-Sharpe risk budgets) was retired after independent review showed plain 1/N beat it on every reported window — the classic estimation-error result.
  • Overlay. A single tail gate: exposure halves when 60-day vol exceeds its trailing 252-day 99th percentile (applied with a 2-day shift, so it is decidable before the fill). No vol target (pure drag under the no-margin cap), no drawdown throttle (the original formula was inert due to a sign bug; the corrected version failed out-of-sample).
  • Gross exposure. Capped at 100% at every layer. VANGUARD's former 1.5× internal gross — financed at 0% in the backtest, impossible in a cash account — was removed; its CAGR drop (22.7%→15.6%) was margin, not edge.
  • Crypto investability. Headline history allocates to crypto only from IBIT's listing (2024-01-11). A spot-proxy reference from 2014 exists in the metrics, clearly labeled NOT INVESTABLE — pre-2024 spot returns were not accessible to an ETF account, and the Grayscale-trust alternative was rejected for premium distortions.

Remaining caveats (cannot be fixed, only disclosed)

  • Structure-level selection bias. Which seven sleeves exist was decided in 2026 with the full 2010–2026 history known in aggregate, and PHOENIX survives a repo of ~25 failed sibling strategies. No walk-forward protocol removes that. Expect live performance below backtest.
  • Sleeve OOS decay is real and shown, not hidden: BND has been net-negative since 2019 (SR −0.09); TOM has been ~flat since 2024. They stay at 1/7 because dropping them after seeing that would be selection; the honest lever is the fixed 1/N cap on their damage.
  • Cost model is thin for TYD/UGL/EDC/YINN/UCO-class names (5 bps modeled; 10–30 bps realistic at the open) and daily re-truing to targets between rebalances is costless in the model.
  • Data-layer heal in progress. Prices fetched before 2026-04-06 are dividend-adjusted; an old fetcher appended raw rows after that date, so the Apr–Jun 2026 sleeve rows carry small, mostly conservative errors (e.g. T-bill ex-div days booked as losses). The first crons after v4 deploy re-derive that window from clean adjusted prices; a validator canary then hard-gates any recurrence.
